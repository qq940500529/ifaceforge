# Community conduct

IfaceForge welcomes respectful participation in issues, discussions and pull requests. Discuss the work, make room for different experiences and give feedback that another person can act on.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats and sharing personal information without consent are grounds for moderation. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation or escalate reports to GitHub. Report abuse through GitHub’s reporting controls. Handle security vulnerabilities through [private reporting](SECURITY.md).

This applies to the IfaceForge repository and its community discussions.
